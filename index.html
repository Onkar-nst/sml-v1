About SML

Rooted in the soil, driven by research

SML Limited, formerly Sulphur Mills Limited, is a global leader in advanced innovative solutions in agriculture. We bring science and passion together to create the solutions the world needs, with expertise spanning soil health, nutrition, biologicals and crop protection.

In a growing world threatened by deteriorating soil health and a changing climate, we push boundaries tirelessly to deliver unprecedented agricultural solutions and do more good for people and the planet. With a presence in over 80 countries, our focus stays fixed on long-term sustainability: prevention is better than cure, and what we eat and how we nourish ourselves are what truly matter.

Our Brand

Reap

The Reap brand is recognised for quality that helps nourish, shield and increase growth and yield. The word sits gracefully intertwined within a leaf, a visual ode to our agri-based mission.

The vibrant green leaf symbolises agriculture’s life force and the ecosystem’s interconnectedness. Reap brings a smile of prosperity to farmers, embodying our commitment to harvesting the rewards of labour and contributing to sustainable growth.

Our Mantra

Growing more from less, for the farmer and the planet alike.

Our products offer better efficacy, efficiency and speed of nutrient uptake, and stay available over a greater period of the crop cycle. That improves productivity and quality, and helps farmers earn sustainable profits.

We always aim to optimise dosage, reduce cost per acre, residues and toxicity, and restrict adverse environmental impacts. Our efficient formulations are largely solvent-free, making them environmentally friendly and sustainable.

Sustainable business growth has been our aim, and we work toward it tirelessly. In doing so, SML is creating a long and significant impact on soil health, farmer incomes and food security, reversing climate change and improving quality of life worldwide.

FAQ

Common questions

SML produces formulations across four families: crop protection (insecticides, fungicides and herbicides), crop nutrition, biologicals and plant growth regulators. Manufacturing covers water dispersible granules (WDG), suspension concentrates (SC) and emulsifiable solutions (EW), with 100,000 MT of WDG capacity, the largest in the world.

More than 80 countries across every continent: the USA, Europe, Australia, the Middle East, South America, Asia and New Zealand. Exports began around three decades ago and now run through strategic alliances, contractual sales arrangements and subsidiary distribution networks. Our most recent expansion was a Brazil office in Campinas, São Paulo, opened in December 2023.

Our formulations are designed to raise nutrient-use efficiency, which reduces dependence on synthetic fertiliser and helps retain soil organic matter and texture. They are also largely solvent-free. SML reports greenhouse-gas emission reductions of 18–84% from these solutions, work recognised by the FICCI Sustainable Agriculture Award for agri-based decarbonization.

Yes. Our Krishinnova farm-technology centres provide seed-to-harvest guidance, Good Agricultural Practices training, soil analysis, weather forecasting, drone spraying and general farm advisory, offered as free-standing support alongside the product range.
